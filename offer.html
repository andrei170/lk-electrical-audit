Two separate problems in one number

First, a third of the site sells domestic work. You told us that is roughly 10% of your business and not the part you want to grow. Every domestic enquiry costs you a call, a quote and, on a job you cannot price competitively against a one-man band, a loss either way.

Second, 617 of those 624 pages are not in your navigation. Only seven pages are reachable from your own menu. The rest exist only in the sitemap, generated from a template with the town name swapped.

We compared two of them earlier this month: they were 99.7% identical, 651 words each, and the only difference between them was the anti-spam sum on the form.

Which is why this is worth doing properly

You are not trying to fill a diary. You are trying to add two or three contracts a month at a value where one of them pays for a year of marketing.

You also told us you can afford a lower conversion rate, because the jobs are large enough to carry it. That is unusual and it is an advantage — it means we can go after fewer, better enquiries rather than chasing volume.

What We Are Not Selling You
Because you have already ruled these out and you were right to
  • Meta advertising. You ran it at the start of the year, it brought low-end work and time wasters, and you found the setup expensive. On £25k commercial contracts it is the wrong instrument — those buyers are not making that decision from a feed.
  • Checkatrade and Rated People. Your words: you outgrew them and they are the wrong demographic. They are domestic consumer platforms and you are a commercial contractor.
